About West Square

The City of Salisbury lists West Square as its oldest local historic district, designated in 1975. The city describes it as mostly residential, with many grand homes built by prominent residents and a few commercial buildings on West Innes Street.

The city names these styles in the district: Federal, Neo-Classical Revival, Greek Revival, Italianate, Queen Anne, Spanish Mission and Craftsman. West Square and the downtown core together make up the National Register's Salisbury Historic District, which was listed in 1975 and extended later.

The exact edges of West Square are drawn on the city's historic district map. If you are not sure your address is inside, ask the city before planning exterior work.

Local rule to check

Before you change the outside of a house

A local historic district adds a review step, called a Certificate of Appropriateness, for changes that show from the street. The city's historic preservation staff can tell you whether your job needs one.

Plumbing jobs that can touch the exterior include a new wall vent for a water heater, a moved meter box and a visible drain or vent line. Ask the city first, then tell the plumber what it said, because the plumber cannot approve exterior changes for you.

Houses of several eras on one street

A district with Federal, Victorian and Craftsman houses has plumbing from different decades, and many houses have been updated over the years. Nobody can tell what is inside a wall from the street, so this page makes no claim about the pipes in any particular house.

What helps is facts. A camera look at the drain, a pressure reading at a hose bib and a check of the shutoff valves show the actual condition, and a good plumber will explain what each result means before suggesting a repair.

Have this ready when you call

A few details make the first conversation faster.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you know where the main shutoff is.
  • Any past plumbing work you know about, including what materials were used.
  • Whether the job could change a vent, meter box or visible pipe outside.

What to do and what not to do while you wait

Simple steps that keep a small problem small.

  • Shut off the water at the nearest valve if a pipe is leaking, then call.
  • Skip chemical drain cleaner on a line that keeps backing up, because it can sit in old pipe and harm the next person who opens it.
  • Hold off on the washer and dishwasher while a drain is slow.
  • Take photos of the damage before cleanup so the repair is easy to review.
  • Do not move a vent, meter box or visible pipe on the outside of a historic house before asking the city.

Water, sewer and the line to your house

Salisbury-Rowan Utilities provides water and sewer service in Salisbury. When a large break happens on a private water line, the city asks the property owner to report it to the utility and to arrange the repair.

The city's page on private line breaks does not say exactly where the utility's line ends and yours begins, so ask the utility about your address. The same page says the account may qualify for an adjustment review after a repair when usage returns to normal.
