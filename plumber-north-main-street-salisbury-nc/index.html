Plumber on North Main Street, Salisbury, NC

North Main Street is a long historic corridor that runs north from the railroad tracks to the Salisbury and Spencer boundary. This page covers plumbing help for the houses and small businesses along it, and how to tell which town your address belongs to.

A corridor, not a block

The City of Salisbury's North Main Street local historic district, designated in 1993, covers homes along North Main Street from the railroad tracks to the Salisbury and Spencer boundary. The National Register district is roughly bounded by N Main, 17th, Lee and Lafayette Streets and covers about 70 acres.

Most of it was developed between about 1900 and 1930, largely for railroad workers, businesspeople and community leaders. The city names Late Victorian, Colonial Revival and Bungalow styles, and the National Register counts 123 contributing buildings.

Streetcars, shops and the Spencer line

The Salisbury and Spencer streetcar line ran from 1905 to 1938, and its cars were kept in a car barn on North Main Street. The line connected Salisbury with the town of Spencer, where Southern Railway built the Spencer Shops in 1896.

The city notes some commercial areas between 12th and Midway Streets. That means the corridor holds both houses and small businesses, and the two have different plumbing needs.

Boundary check

Salisbury or Spencer? Check before you plan permits

Spencer is a separate town with its own government, even though the two towns share the same water and sewer utility. Near the boundary, the town that issues permits depends on which side your property is on.

If you are close to the line, confirm your jurisdiction with the town before starting work. We cover both, and our Spencer page has local detail for the other side.

What to do when something goes wrong

A corridor with houses and shops has more than one kind of urgent problem. For a house, shut off the water at the fixture valve or the main and call. For a shop, tell the building owner or manager as well, because a lease often decides who approves the repair.

Whatever the building, ask for the price and the scope in plain terms before work begins, and ask what the plumber found before anything is replaced.

Before you call about a North Main house or shop

  • The address and whether it is in Salisbury or Spencer.
  • Whether the building is a house, a shop or both.
  • What you see and when it started.
  • Where the main shutoff is, if you know.
  • Who owns the building, if you rent.
