Spencer homes and the pipes inside them

Spencer was incorporated in 1905 and named for Samuel Spencer, the Southern Railway president whose company built the Spencer Shops in 1896. The town's Spencer Historic District holds early twentieth-century homes, and houses from that period often still carry older drain lines and original supply pipe.

That matters on a service call. A slow drain in an old house can be a failing cast iron line rather than a simple clog, so we start with drain cleaning and move to a camera inspection when the problem keeps returning.

Parks, museums and businesses we serve in Spencer

Spencer Town Park, the North Carolina Transportation Museum on the former shops site and the NC Museum of Dolls, Toys and Miniatures make the town a destination. Businesses around them need restrooms, hot water and drains that work during open hours.
