Where Park Avenue is

A Salisbury Post article from 2011 roughly bounds the neighborhood by East Innes Street, Interstate 85, Bringle Ferry Road and Long Street, with downtown only a few blocks away. Because the article is the main source for those edges, treat them as approximate.

The article says Park Avenue was once home to well-off families and a large mill village. Since 1998 the city and the Park Avenue Redevelopment Corporation have added a community center, two parks and an EMS station.

A mix of owners, renters and house types

The 2011 article describes a diverse housing stock, and it reported that more than three-quarters of residents rented at the time of the 2000 Census. Those figures are old, and the neighborhood has changed since, so use them only as background.

Some newer owners have restored larger older houses, and that is a good reminder that the street holds very different buildings. A plumber should look at the building in front of them and not assume anything from the neighborhood name. Common starting points are leak detection, drain cleaning and water heater repair.

City program

Help with water bills is not the same as help with repairs

The City of Salisbury runs Share2Care, a water and sewer bill assistance program for Salisbury-Rowan Utilities customers facing financial hardship. It is funded by voluntary customer donations, and applications go through Rowan Helping Ministries.

The city's page describes help with utility bills, not plumbing repairs, and the program is open to utility customers across the area and not only to Park Avenue. After a large leak is repaired, ask the utility about an adjustment review for the bill.
