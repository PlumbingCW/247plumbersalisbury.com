Seasonal tip

Pools, parks and outdoor plumbing in Landis

Homes near Lake Corriher Wilderness Park and the Landis Pool often have outdoor spigots, irrigation lines and hose bibs that see heavy use in summer. A hose left attached through winter can freeze and split the pipe behind the wall.

Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze, and tell us if you notice a drip at the spigot. A small repair now prevents a burst later.

Code notes for plumbing in Landis

Plumbing work in Landis follows the North Carolina plumbing code, which is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and electrical work for pumps follows the National Electrical Code (NEC).
