How to Install an Under Sink Water Filtration System in Salisbury, NC

An under-sink filter treats the water you drink and cook with at one tap. The best choice depends on what you want to remove, so start by reading your local water report.

By the Salisbury 24/7 Plumbing team.Published October 23, 2026.6 min read.

What an under-sink system does

Most under-sink filters connect to the cold supply line and send water through a carbon block or a multi-stage cartridge, then to a small faucet or to the main faucet through a diverter. Some add reverse osmosis membranes for finer filtration.

Choose by what you want to reduce, not by brand. Look for a system certified to the ANSI/NSF standard for the contaminant you care about, such as lead, chlorine taste or particulates.

Installation overview

Choose the location

Find space under the sink and a path for tubing.

Connect the supply

Add a tee or adapter to the cold shutoff valve.

Mount the filter

Secure the housing to the cabinet wall.

Flush and test

Run water through the filter and check for leaks.

Reading a certification label

Look for the standard number on the package, such as the ANSI/NSF standards for specific contaminants, and read which contaminants the product is certified to reduce. A general claim like 'removes impurities' is not a certification.

Certified products list what they reduce and by how much, and independent testers publish those results.

Questions that guide the choice

What are you filtering for?

Taste, lead, sediment or something else.

Do you want a separate faucet?

Many systems include one.

How much cabinet space do you have?

Reverse osmosis units are larger.

How often will you change cartridges?

Plan for a schedule.

Decision point

Do you need a separate faucet?

Most under-sink systems come with a small dedicated faucet that mounts in a spare sink hole or a drilled hole in the countertop. Some connect to a diverter on the main faucet instead.

A dedicated faucet gives you filtered water without running the main tap, but it means drilling stone or composite counters. Ask before you commit.

Space under the sink

A kitchen sink cabinet already holds a disposal, a trap, supply lines and often a dishwasher connection. A filter housing and cartridges need to fit as well, with room to change them.

Measure the space, including the height needed to remove the cartridge. A system you cannot service is a system you will not service.

What Salisbury-area water reports say

Salisbury-Rowan Utilities draws water from the Yadkin River and treats it at a plant near downtown. East Spencer's published report lists soft water with hardness near 23 ppm, so a softener is not a priority for most homes served this way.

The China Grove water report from July 2025 advises flushing pipes and considering an ANSI-certified filter, because lead results from sampled homes reached the action level. A filter at the kitchen tap is a reasonable step for homes with older plumbing, and a certified lab can test your own water.

Cartridge care

Filters only help while they are fresh.

  • Mark the install date on the housing.
  • Replace cartridges on the maker's schedule.
  • Flush a new cartridge as directed.
  • Keep a spare on hand.
  • Sanitize the system when you change cartridges if the maker recommends it.

When to call a plumber

A simple carbon filter is a good do-it-yourself project. A reverse osmosis system, a filter that needs a drain connection or one that requires drilling a countertop is easier with a plumber.

If your supply valves are old and will not close, replace them first. We do that as part of faucet and sink repair, and it makes any filter install safer.

A simple annual routine

Pick a date each year, such as the start of a season, to change the cartridge and check each connection. Wipe the housing dry and watch for beads of moisture after you restore pressure.

A filter that is changed on schedule protects your water. One left for years becomes a place for sediment to collect and can slow the flow.

Water pressure and flow

A filter adds resistance, and a clogged cartridge slows the flow at the tap. If you notice weaker flow, change the cartridge before you suspect the faucet.

Reverse osmosis systems produce water slowly and store it in a small tank. Plan your use around that, and do not expect the same flow as the main faucet. If you fill large pots often, a carbon block system gives faster flow.

Filters in older homes around Salisbury

Homes with galvanized or old copper pipe can have more particles and metals reaching the tap. A filter helps with that, but it does not replace dealing with failing pipe.

In China Grove and Landis, we often suggest a filter alongside a look at older service lines, and a repiping discussion if pipes are failing.

Code check

Codes and standards worth knowing

Look for filters certified to ANSI/NSF standards for the contaminant you care about, and install them with connections that meet the International Plumbing Code (IPC).
