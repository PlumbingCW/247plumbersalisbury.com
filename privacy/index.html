Who we are

Salisbury 24/7 Plumbing operates this website and a plumbing help line serving Salisbury, NC. You can reach us at (704) 951-1635 or contact@247plumbersalisbury.com.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, address and a description of your plumbing problem so that we can schedule and perform the work.

We use this information to provide service, to follow up on work and to improve how we answer calls. We do not sell it.

This website

This website does not have contact forms or user accounts. We do not ask you to type personal information into the site.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, for security and to keep the site running.

Embedded maps

Our pages include embedded Google Maps. Google may collect information when the map loads, under its own privacy policy. You can avoid this by not scrolling to the map section or by blocking embedded content in your browser.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing contact@247plumbersalisbury.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
