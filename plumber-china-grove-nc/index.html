How China Grove got its name and why the housing is mixed

China Grove was chartered on March 11, 1889 and takes its name from a chinaberry grove near the depot. Before that, the area was known as Savits Mill and Lutherville. In 1896, the town had the first Rural Free Delivery route in North Carolina, started on October 23.

With a 2020 population of 4,434, China Grove has homes from many eras. Older houses may have galvanized or aging copper supply lines, and our repiping page explains when replacement beats repair.

Reading your water report

China Grove buys treated water from Salisbury-Rowan Utilities and publishes its own water quality report. The July 2025 report listed lead at the 15 ppb action level at the 90th percentile, with two sampled homes above it and a range from none detected to 59 ppb. The report advises flushing pipes and considering an ANSI-certified filter, and it points to the utility for sampling data.

That report describes a sample of homes, not yours. We do not test water, but we can look for old service line or fixture materials and discuss replacing them, and a certified lab can test your tap.

Practical note

Flushing and filters: what the report suggests in practice

The water report for China Grove recommends running cold water to flush pipes that have sat unused, such as after a vacation, and considering an ANSI-certified filter. Flushing for a minute or two before drinking or cooking is free and easy.

If you install a filter, choose one certified for lead reduction and replace cartridges on schedule. We can fit a filter at the kitchen sink when we are on site for another repair.

Older fixtures and shutoffs in China Grove homes

Homes from the early and middle twentieth century often have original shutoff valves that no longer close, and brass or chrome fixtures from decades ago. A valve that will not close turns a small repair into a whole-house shutdown.

When we repair a leak, we check the nearby shutoffs and tell you which ones to replace. It is a small cost that makes the next emergency much easier.

Code notes for plumbing in China Grove

Replacement pipe and fittings in China Grove must be approved for potable water under the International Plumbing Code (IPC) as adopted in North Carolina.

For drinking water filters, look for ANSI/NSF certification for the contaminant you want reduced.
