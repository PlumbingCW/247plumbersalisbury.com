What granite means for plumbing

The town was incorporated in 1901 and went by Woodville and Woodside before the name Granite Quarry was approved in 1905. Large-scale quarrying began in 1906, and the Old Stone House, built in 1766 by Michael Braun, still stands.

Where granite sits close to the surface, trenching for a water line or a sewer lateral is slower and may need different equipment. That affects how we quote sewer line repair and water line repair here, and we tell you before work starts.
